# Events

## Drake DefenseCon

### Anvil Armor Display

> Rising to prominence as one of the UEE’s most trusted makers of military hardware over the last two centuries, their clarity of vision and unmatched quality has made an indelible mark on Humanity’s history.

### Drake Armor Display

> With a passionate following of loyal pilots who love their no-nonsense approach to spacecraft, this sometimes-controversial ship manufacturer continues to defy expectations.

### Kruger Armor Display

> What began as a renowned precision parts manufacturer has grown to become the maker of elegantly designed and meticulously crafted spaceships. 

### Murray Cup Armor Display

> Unquestionably the premier racing league in the empire, it has made household names of skilled pilots who are quick off the line, a deft hand at tight turns, and ready to push beyond the limits.

### Nine Tails

> Despite their growing reputation, little is known publicly about the gang or its inner workings; making the group’s meteoric rise to power only more worrisome to organized crime experts. Today, the Nine Tails gang operates almost exclusively within the Stanton system, using the infamous derelict space station known among locals as ‘Grim HEX’ as its headquarters.

### Shattered Blade

> One of the most significant suppliers of black-market alien tech, these smugglers have been escalating tensions in Nyx thanks to their aggressive methodologies that have been drawing the ire of the Vanduul and increasing raids across the system. 

### XenoThreat

> Preaching a viciously anti-UEE and xenophobic rhetoric, XenoThreat forces have used their hideouts in Pyro to launch dozens of attacks against civilian and corporate structures across the empire in pursuit of their Human-first agenda.

### Vanduul

> This aggressive alien species composed of independent warrior clans has been plaguing the empire for centuries with their brutal raids on Human systems and settlements. In 2945, increased hostilities following the Battle of Vega have led the UEE to formally declare war against these cruel and sadistic invaders.
