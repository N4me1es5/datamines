## Armor

### Tailwind Flight Helmet

> Item Type: Flight Helmet  
> Damage Reduction: 15%  
> Temp. Rating: -30 / 60 °C  
> Radiation Protection: 15200 REM  
> Radiation Scrub Rate: 81 REM/s  
>  
> Styled on classic racing helmets, the Tailwind helmet offers crucial insulation and neck support from the rigors of the racing cockpit. The double layered visor provides essential protection from glare whilst ensuring the helmet remains airtight and that the wearer's vision remains unimpeded.

**Variants**
* **Adrenaline:** The Adrenaline edition is a crisp white offset against a gray mouthpiece, finished with striking red lines. 
* **Airwolf:** The Airwolf edition consists of a ferocious white wolf that pops against a navy blue and crimson red helmet. 
* **Big Bite:** The Big Bite edition features jagged teeth and menacing eyes set against a camo green. 
* **Blackbolt:** Featuring lightning and a chevron pattern, the Blackbolt edition splits the helmet in two with a contrasting bright yellow and black. 
* **Blue Skies:** The Blue Skies edition fuses shades of blue and white together with an emblem emblazoned on each side. 
* **Frostshock:** Featuring stripes and an electric pattern, the Frostshock edition utilizes a chilling blue against an icy white. 
* **Green Streak:** The Green Streak edition combines a racing green, sharp yellow stripes, and a ship flying over the crown of the helmet. 
* **Thunderstruck:** The Thunderstruck edition features black lightning bolts, stars, and stripes, that leap out against a sunset orange. 

### Tailwind Flight Suit

> Item Type: Flight Suit  
> Damage Reduction: 15%  
> Temp. Rating: -30 / 60 °C  
> Radiation Protection: 15200 REM  
> Radiation Scrub Rate: 81 REM/s  
> G-Force Tolerance: +97.5%  
>  
> Taking its design cues from the early days of aviation, the Tailwind flight suit prioritizes functionality above all. Utilizing a thin layer of woolen insulation, a quilted collar, and breathable materials in key locations, this suit gives every pilot the best chance at success.

### Tailwind Flight Suit Sets (Both Helmet and Suit)

* **Anvil:** Tough, sleek, and industrial, the Anvil edition emphasizes the vehicle manufacturer's values with a military green base, sharp yellow stripes, and the company's logo emblazoned proudly on it. 
* **Dominion Camo:** The Dominion Camo edition adds a dark green camo pattern to the flight helmet. 
* **Drake:** Show your pride for the company that champions the everyman with the Drake edition of the Tailwind helmet. 
* **Kruger:** Featuring a sophisticated gray with refined brass accents, the Kruger edition of this flight suit exemplifies the high quality and detail oriented focus of the ship company. 
* **Murray Cup:** Utilizing the neon yellows and vivid blues associated with the Murray Cup, this special edition features the logos of the official sponsors of the race.
